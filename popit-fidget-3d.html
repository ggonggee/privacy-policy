This privacy policy applies to the app "Popit Fidget 3D" (com.ggonggee.popit3d) published by HUHI game on Google Play.

1. Information we collect

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or files. Game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

The advertising services built into the app (Unity LevelPlay and its ad network partners, including Unity Ads and Google AdMob) may collect and process:

  • Device or other identifiers - Android advertising ID and similar device identifiers
  • Approximate location - derived from the IP address

2. Why it is used

  • To show advertisements and measure their performance
  • To prevent ad fraud

3. Sharing with third parties

The information above is shared only with the following service providers, who process it under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity LevelPlay / Unity Ads - unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob - policies.google.com/privacy
  • Other ad networks mediated by LevelPlay - ironSource mobile privacy policy

These providers may be located outside your country (including the United States).

4. Security and retention

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). HUHI game does not store this data on its own servers. Retention is governed by each ad provider's policy.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • You can stop all collection by uninstalling the app.
  • You may request access to or deletion of any data about you by emailing huhigame00@gmail.com. HUHI game does not hold such data on its own servers; requests about advertising data can also be made directly to the providers in Section 3.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact huhigame00@gmail.com and we will ask the ad providers to delete it.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change. Questions: huhigame00@gmail.com
